Adults: Start or Restart

Progress with patient, structured practice and practical bike-fit.

Setup and mindset

  • Fit: low seat to start, comfortable reach, flat pedals, easy gears.
  • Mindset: small wins, consistent short sessions, fear is normal.
  • Environment: flat smooth area; add gentle slopes later.

Skills progression

  1. Balance: scoot and glide, eyes up; progress to gentle steering.
  2. Pedal starts: power foot at 2 o’clock, push and glide.
  3. Gearing: easy cadence; shift before slow climbs or stops.
  4. Turns & control: wide to tighter turns; controlled braking.

Common hurdles

  • Fear of falling: practice near grass edge, use gloves.
  • Oversteer: look through the turn; light hands, steady core.
  • Fatigue: keep sessions 20–30 min; hydrate and rest.
