Kids: Learn to Ride

Make it fun, safe, and confidence-building with short, playful sessions.

Before you start

  • Fit the bike: stand-over clearance, reach, and seat slightly low.
  • Helmet fit: level, snug, Y-straps under ears, 2-finger brow gap.
  • Choose a flat, traffic-free place: park path, empty lot, or gym.

Progression (3–6 sessions)

  1. Glide: Remove pedals, scoot and glide feet up for 3–5 seconds.
  2. Steer: Weave around cones/chalk, look where you want to go.
  3. Brake: Squeeze both gently; stop at a line on cue.
  4. Pedal: Reinstall pedals; power foot at 2 o’clock start.
  5. Turn & stop: Large circles, then figure-8s; predictable stops.

Make it fun

  • Use games: “red light/green light”, follow-the-leader, treasure route.
  • Keep sessions short (15–25 min) and end on a win.
  • Celebrate progress, not speed or distance.
