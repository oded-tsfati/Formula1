Seniors: Comfortable, Confident Riding

Emphasize comfort, balance, gradual progress, and assistive options.

Comfort & setup

  • Upright position: higher bars, wider saddle, suspension seatpost.
  • Low-step frame: easier mounts; consider e-bike for assist.
  • Tires: wider at lower pressure for comfort and grip.

Balance & safety

  1. Mount/dismount practice next to a rail or wall.
  2. Coasting and gentle turns in a quiet area.
  3. Controlled braking; avoid front-only stops.

Health considerations

  • Check with a clinician for cardiac, balance, or joint issues.
  • Start with 10–15 min rides; increase slowly.
  • Use lights and bright clothing for visibility at all times.
