Bike sizing quick guide

  • Kids: wheel size often matches height; test straddle and reach.
  • Adults: start with frame size charts, then test-ride for comfort.
  • Raise saddle to hip height over time for efficient pedaling.

Recommended gear

  • Helmet certified (CPSC/EN1078) with proper fit.
  • Front white and rear red rechargeable lights.
  • Bell, flat kit (tube/patches/levers/pump), gloves.

Find local classes

Search for “Learn to Ride” at your local bike coalition, community center, or bike shop. Many offer adult-first-timers sessions and family clinics.
