Safety Essentials

Quick checks and predictable riding keep you safe.

ABC quick check

  • A — Air: Tires firm; check pressure sidewall.
  • B — Brakes: Squeeze both; pads align; levers firm.
  • C — Chain/Cranks/Cassette: Chain clean and lubed; no wobble.

Be predictable

  • Signal turns/stops; make eye contact at intersections.
  • Ride with traffic; obey signals and signs.
  • Stay out of the door zone; take the lane when needed.

Visibility

  • Front white and rear red lights at dusk/dawn and poor weather.
  • Reflective details on bike and clothing help drivers see you.
  • Choose routes with separation or calm traffic where possible.
