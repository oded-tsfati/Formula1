Teens: Ride with Confidence

Sharpen control, awareness, and responsibility on paths and roads.

Core drills

  • Slow-speed handling: track stand attempts, tight U-turns, slalom.
  • Braking practice: progressive braking, emergency stops from 10–15 km/h.
  • Signaling and scanning: look back while riding straight, then signal.

Street skills

  1. Route planning: calm streets, bike lanes, safe crossings.
  2. Positioning: take the lane when narrow; avoid the door zone.
  3. Right-of-way: intersections, roundabouts, predictable communication.

Group riding basics

  • Ride single file unless space allows; verbal/hand signals for hazards.
  • Keep safe following distance; no sudden braking or weaving.
  • Be visible: lights at dusk/dawn, reflective details.
